Who we are

SONIC: Your success starts here. Production and marketing under one roof.

Founded in 2022, SONIC YO9 brings marketing, photography, filmmaking and fashion retail together under one roof. Hiring one agency for campaigns and another studio for visuals means endless coordination. We removed that gap: the strategy and the creative are built together, by one team, and measured against the same goals.

SONIC is behind several companies and brands. We work with a wide network of photographers and videographers, from established studios to independent freelancers, and choose the right team for each project. Credits shown on the work belong to the photographers who made it.

Pricing depends on the scope: platforms, number of shoots and deliverables. After an intro call we send a written proposal.

Edited photos and films, with video delivered in up to 4K. Vertical cuts for Reels and TikTok are available when you need them.
